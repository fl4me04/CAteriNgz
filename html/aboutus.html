About Us

CAteriNgz is a distinguished homemade culinary service that has become a staple for exceptional food at various events across Jakarta, Bandung, and Malang. Our journey began in a humble kitchen, driven by a passion for cooking and a dedication to quality. Over the years, our commitment to creating memorable dining experiences has earned us a reputation for excellence. We cater to all types of gatherings, from intimate family celebrations to grand corporate events, ensuring that each occasion is marked by delectable cuisine and impeccable service.
At CAteriNgz, we pride ourselves on using the finest ingredients available. Our dedication to quality starts with sourcing our produce locally, ensuring that we support the community and promote sustainability. By using seasonal ingredients, we guarantee that our dishes are always fresh and bursting with flavor. Our chefs are passionate about their craft, working closely with local farmers and artisans to bring out the best in every ingredient. This collaboration not only enhances the taste of our food but also highlights the rich culinary heritage of our regions.
The culinary creations at CAteriNgz are a testament to our commitment to excellence and innovation. Our menu is a harmonious blend of traditional flavors and modern culinary techniques, designed to delight and surprise our clients. Whether it’s a sophisticated canapé for a cocktail party, a sumptuous buffet for a wedding, or a themed meal for a corporate event, each dish is meticulously crafted to meet our high standards. Our team is dedicated to providing a seamless and enjoyable experience, ensuring that every event we cater is remembered for its outstanding food and exceptional service.
